Fake job scam guide

How to spot a scam, how to verify an offer, and what to do if you have been targeted.

8 warning signs

1. They ask for money

Registration, training, kit or "refundable" deposit. Real employers never charge you to hire you.

2. Documents too early

Aadhaar, PAN, bank or card details before any official offer letter is a big red flag.

3. Free email address

Hiring emails from gmail.com or yahoo.com instead of a company domain.

4. Only WhatsApp or Telegram

No official email, no video call, no company portal.

5. Unreal salary

Big guaranteed earnings for simple work or a couple of hours a day.

6. No interview

"Direct joining", "no experience needed", "anyone can apply".

7. Pressure

"Limited seats", "apply immediately", lots of exclamation marks.

8. Vague company

No website, no address, no real people on LinkedIn, copy-pasted job text.

How to verify an offer

  1. Search the company name plus the word "scam" or "reviews".
  2. Open the company's official website yourself. Do not use links from the message.
  3. Find the same job on the company's careers page.
  4. Check that the recruiter's email uses the company domain.
  5. Look up the recruiter on LinkedIn and see if they really work there.
  6. Ask for a video or in-person interview. Genuine firms agree.

If you think you were scammed

  1. Stop all contact and do not send more money or documents.
  2. Save screenshots, chats, payment receipts and phone numbers.
  3. Tell your bank at once if you paid or shared banking details.
  4. In India, report on the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal (cybercrime.gov.in) or call the helpline 1930.
  5. Report the posting on the platform where you found it.
  6. Warn friends and your college placement cell.
