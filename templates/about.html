About JobShield

A student-built tool that explains why a job posting looks risky.

How the detection works

  1. Text cleaning. The posting is lowercased and simplified.
  2. TF-IDF. Words and word pairs are turned into numbers based on how distinctive they are.
  3. Logistic Regression. A classifier trained on labelled real and fake postings gives a probability.
  4. Rule engine. Pattern rules look for fees, ID requests, chat-app contact and similar tricks.
  5. Hybrid score. The two scores are combined. A very serious rule can raise the final risk by itself.
  6. Explanation. We show which flags and words influenced the result.

Limits you should know

  • The model learns from example postings, so new scam styles can slip through.
  • A "Looks safe" result is not a guarantee. Always verify the employer.
  • It works best on English postings.
  • It never replaces your own judgement. If anything feels off, stop.
